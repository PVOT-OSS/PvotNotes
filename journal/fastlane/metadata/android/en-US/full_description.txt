Journal is a private diary for your days: what happened, what you thought, and the photos that go with it.

Write about your day
• Each entry has a date and time, a title and your text
• Write in Markdown and read it formatted, with checklists you can tick
• Add photos from your gallery to any entry
• Tags to bring related entries together
• Everything saves as you type

Look back
• A timeline grouped by day, week or month
• A week strip with a dot on every day you wrote
• Tap a day to see only its entries, or write one for it

Quick to capture
• A quick entry field at the top of the timeline
• Share text or a photo from any app into a new entry
• A "New entry" shortcut on your home screen

Private and open
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking
• No internet permission: your journal stays on your phone
• Photos are copied into the app, so your entries keep them
• Free and open source under the Apache-2.0 licence: github.com/PVOT-OSS/Notes
