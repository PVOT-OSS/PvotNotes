# Privacy Policy — Notes

_Effective 11 November 2026_

Notes (package `org.prauga.notes`) is a note-taking app published by Saalim Quadri for PVOT-OSS.
This policy explains what the app does with your data.

**In short: the app doesn't collect, send, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no
ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no permission to use the internet.**

## What the app keeps on your device

- **Your notes**: each note's title, text, tags, colour and collection, when it was created and last
  changed, and an internal identifier and revision number.
- **Your collections**: their names and colours.
- **A setting**: whether the list shows notes as a grid or a list.

All of this stays in the app's private storage. Deleting a note or a collection removes it from the
app straight away (deleting a collection also deletes its notes). Everything is deleted when you
clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

## Backups

If you turn on Android's device backup, Android may include your notes and the app's setting in your
device backup or in a transfer to a new device (for example, Google's backup on most devices), under
the terms of that backup service. The app itself never sends them anywhere. You can turn backup off
in your device's settings.

## Sharing

- When you share text or a link from another app to Notes, the app saves it as a new note on your
  device.
- When you share a note, the app passes its title and text to the app you pick in Android's share
  sheet. What happens to it there is up to that app.

Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

## Links

When you tap a link in a note, Android opens it in another app, such as your browser. Notes itself
doesn't load anything from the internet.

## The home screen shortcut

The app adds a "New note" shortcut to your launcher and tells Android when you use it, so the
launcher can rank its shortcuts. This stays on your device.

## Permissions

The app requests no permissions. The only one listed in the app,
`org.prauga.notes.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION`, is added by the AndroidX library to keep
the app's internal broadcasts private; it gives the app no access to your data.

The app does not have the `INTERNET` permission.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is
released, with a new effective date at the top.
