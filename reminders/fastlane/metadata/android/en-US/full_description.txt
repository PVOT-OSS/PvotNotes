Reminders keeps your to-dos in order and reminds you on time, without accounts, ads or tracking.

Plan your day
• Tasks are grouped into Overdue, Today, Upcoming and No date
• Due dates with an optional time, set in a tap: Today, Tomorrow or any date
• Priority: low, medium or high, shown with a flag and a label
• Subtasks with a running count on the list
• Notes on any task

Reminders that arrive on time
• One or more reminders per task: at the due time, 10 minutes, an hour or a day before
• Exact alarms, so a reminder fires at the minute you set
• Mark a task done or snooze it for 10 minutes straight from the notification
• Reminders come back after a restart, and snoozes too

Repeating tasks
• Daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly or yearly
• Completing a repeating task moves it to its next date

Quick to capture
• A quick add field at the top of the list
• Share text from any app into a new task
• A "New reminder" shortcut on your home screen

Private and open
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking
• No internet permission: your tasks stay on your phone
• Free and open source under the Apache-2.0 licence: github.com/PVOT-OSS/Notes
