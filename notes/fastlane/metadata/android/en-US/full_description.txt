Notes is a clean, private place for everything you want to keep: lists, ideas, plans and drafts.

Write in Markdown, read it formatted
• Headings, bold, italic, strikethrough, code, quotes, numbered and bulleted lists, and links
• Notes open formatted; tap the text or the pencil to edit
• Checklists: write "- [ ] item" and tick it straight in the note
• Everything saves as you type

Find things fast
• Search the titles and text of all your notes as you type
• Tags: add as many as you like and filter the list by one
• Colours to make notes stand out
• Collections to keep work and personal notes apart
• Grid or list view

Quick to capture
• A quick note field at the top of the list
• Share text or a link from any app into a new note
• Share any note out as text
• A "New note" shortcut on your home screen

Private and open
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking
• No internet permission: your notes stay on your phone
• Free and open source under the Apache-2.0 licence: github.com/PVOT-OSS/Notes
